What happens next

  1. A recurring defect sets the asset to NEEDS_MAINTENANCE and dispatches a service ticket to the campus technician.
  2. A broken item is inspected by staff. If it is repairable and BER is below 50%, it goes UNDER_REPAIR and returns with the same asset ID.
  3. Otherwise it is CONDEMNED / BER and replaced through a verified replacement.
